01 / OVERVIEW

From reviewing drawings
to sharing comments.

Review drawings on the shop floor, in meetings or while away from your desk. Quick2D Viewer brings drawing review and location-based comments into one workflow.

  1. 01 / OPEN

    Open

    Import PDF, DXF or DWG files. Zoom and pan to inspect the area you need.

  2. 02 / COMMENT

    Comment

    Add numbered pins to points of interest. Edit or delete comments as your review develops.

  3. 03 / SHARE

    Share

    Export a PDF containing pin numbers and a comment list, then choose a destination in the iOS share sheet.

02 / FEATURES

Keep drawings and comments together.

See the drawing

Pinch to zoom, drag to pan and use Fit to see the drawing as a whole. Switch pages in multi-page PDFs and toggle layers in CAD drawings.

Leave comments in context

Place numbered comment pins on a drawing or PDF page. Save the text and positions, then reopen the same document stored in the app to review them.

Share numbered comments as a PDF

Create a PDF with pin numbers on the drawing and a list of numbers, pages and comment text. Choose how to share it using the iOS share sheet.

Keep the original file unchanged

Work with imported copies and comments stored on your device. Review and record comments without overwriting the original PDF, DXF or DWG.

03 / FILE FORMATS

PDF, DXF and DWG.

PDF

View multi-page drawings and place comment pins on individual pages.

DXF

View 2D model space in ASCII (text) DXF files. Binary DXF is not supported.

DWG

Read R11/R12, R14, 2000, 2004, 2007, 2010, 2013 and 2018 save formats. R13 and unknown formats are not supported.

CAD viewing is limited to model space. Hatches, external references, CAD fonts and dimensions have limitations. Full reproduction of every element is not guaranteed.

Read the supported scope and CAD viewing limitations →

04 / SHARING

Point to the place. Share the context.

Combine numbered pins and a comment list in a single PDF so others can see which part of the drawing you mean. PDF sources export all pages; DXF and DWG export the currently visible layers fitted onto one model-space page.

Shared PDFs are for review and communication. They do not synchronize or transfer editable comment data between devices. CAD output does not guarantee true-scale printing or manufacturing scale. Drawing geometry editing and dimension measurement are not available.
