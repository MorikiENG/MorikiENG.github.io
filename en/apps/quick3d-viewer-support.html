Support

Supported file formats

STEP / STP, IGES / IGS, STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, GLB / glTF and USDZ / USD are supported. Parts, colors and unit interpretation depend on the format and the file.

Mesh support focuses on static geometry and basic colors. Support for textures, transparency and animation is limited.

Viewing and basic controls

  • Use touch controls to rotate, zoom and pan, and fit the whole model into view.
  • Select parts in the model tree and toggle their visibility.
  • In V2.0, tools appear according to the current context, such as viewing, the model tree, measurement or analysis.
  • Reopen files from a recent history of up to 10 files.
  • Full-screen viewing hides the viewer banner ad.

Measurement and CAD analysis (V2.0)

  • Measurement: point-to-point distance, diameter, radius, plane distance and cylindrical axis distance. Availability depends on geometry and recognition results.
  • Bounding dimensions: inspect model-coordinate X, Y and Z dimensions for the whole model or a selected Body in mm or inches.
  • Volume, mass and center of gravity: available for a valid single closed solid. Calculate mass using a density preset or a custom density and display the center of gravity.
  • 3D annotations: add pins, leaders and comments, save them locally and display them again.
  • Sections: adjust direction and position to preview a section and inspect section lines on B-Rep models.
  • Draft and undercut analysis: inspect direction-based coloring and sampled candidates for straight-pull undercuts.
  • STEP shape comparison: select Bodies within an assembly or from another file to view removed, added and common regions in different colors.

Measurement and analysis limits

  • B-Rep analysis applies to STEP / IGES. Some CAD-specific measurements and analyses are unavailable for mesh formats.
  • Volume, mass and shape comparison require valid closed solids. The app does not automatically calculate a total assembly volume with overlapping regions removed.
  • Shape comparison targets single closed solids in the same coordinate system. Comparison and capped sections are limited to 2,000 faces per target; CPU section previews accept up to 100,000 input triangles.
  • Draft and undercut analysis is approximate and may miss small features. It does not guarantee manufacturability.
  • Density-based mass is a reference value. Verify important geometry, dimensions and analysis results against the original CAD or reference documentation.
  • Uncertain units are treated as assumed or unknown. Point-to-point measurement may also be unavailable for USD files without identifiable units.

Troubleshooting

  • A file will not open: check the formats above and confirm the download to your device is complete.
  • Measurement or analysis is unavailable: check the format, target Body geometry, whether it is a closed solid, unit information and the limits above.
  • Comparison results do not match: confirm that the targets use matching coordinate systems and placement.
  • Controls have changed: V2.0 shows tools according to the current context. Include your app version when contacting support.

Contact

Use “Quick3D Viewer” in the subject and include:

  • App version, device model and iOS/iPadOS version
  • File format and steps to reproduce the issue
  • Any error message or a screenshot you can safely share

moriki.engineering@gmail.com

You do not need to attach confidential drawings or personal information in an initial inquiry. If a sample file is needed, discuss a reproducible example that you have permission to share.

Data and advertising

CAD file loading and analysis run on your device, without uploading files to a developer-operated analysis server. History and annotations are stored locally. The app uses AdMob advertising and UMP consent handling; see the privacy policy for details.
