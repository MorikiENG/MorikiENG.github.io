Quick3D Viewer

3D CAD Viewing, Made Simple.

A free 3D viewer developed by Moriki Engineering for iPhone and iPad. Open CAD and 3D models in a range of formats, wherever you work. Formerly known as QuickSTEP Viewer.

Download on the App StoreSupport & privacy

About the app

Version 1.1 introduces the Quick3D Viewer name and adds STL, OBJ, PLY, 3MF, GLB and USDZ to STEP and IGES support. It also adds a full-screen mode and improves layouts for iPhone and iPad.

Based on the App Store listing for version 1.1, checked on October 15, 2026.

Key features

  • Open CAD and 3D models in multiple file formats.
  • Rotate, zoom and pan models with touch gestures.
  • Use full-screen mode to view models with banner ads hidden.
  • View models with layouts optimized for iPhone and iPad.

Supported file formats

  • STEP / STP
  • IGES / IGS
  • STL
  • OBJ
  • PLY
  • 3MF
  • GLB
  • USDZ

Formats listed in the App Store description for version 1.1.
