StickyCam ふせんカメラ

Support

StickyCam is under development. This guide describes the reviewed development version. Advertising, consent management and the final caption specifications are not yet finalized.

Save a photo with a memo

  1. Allow camera access and permission to add photos.
  2. Enter a memo and adjust the note’s position and appearance.
  3. Take a photo and check that saving completes. View the result in your device’s standard photo library.

Memo compositing runs on your device. The app does not request permission to read existing photos.

Manage history and photos

Memos used in successfully saved photos are kept on your device in a history of up to 50 entries. Delete individual entries or clear all history in the history screen. Retry if a save error appears.

Deleting history does not remove text embedded in photos or previously saved captions. Photos in the standard library remain after the app is deleted. Manage photos and metadata in Photos or other appropriate tools.

If saving fails

  • Check camera and photo-add permissions in device settings.
  • Check available storage and any error message.
  • If the issue continues, contact us with the app version, device, OS and steps to reproduce it.
