1. Operator and this provisional policy

Moriki Engineering (森樹エンジニアリング, the “developer”) explains how information is handled in the iOS/iPadOS app ふせんカメラ (StickyCam). The app is planned to be free and does not require account registration. It has no developer-operated server or proprietary cloud storage.

This is a provisional policy for an app under development. The source reviewed on November 27, 2026 implements on-device photo and memo processing and storage. Advertising SDKs, ATT and UMP are not implemented; advertising is planned for a later development phase. The caption feature has not been finalized for release. We will review the implementation and disclosures and update this policy before release.

2. Camera and photo library access

The app requests camera permission to take photos. Camera footage and captured images are not sent to a developer-operated server. You can change camera permission in your device settings.

The app uses add-only photo library permission to save captured photos to the standard photo library. It does not request permission to read existing photos and does not retrieve or collect photos already in the library. Saving requires permission to add photos.

3. Photo and memo processing

Memos are composited onto captured images on your device. The app has no process that collects photos or memos for the developer or uploads them to proprietary cloud storage. Our policy is not to provide photo or memo contents to advertising providers.

Please review and manage the information in your photos and memos. If they contain personal or confidential information, consider that content, including memos embedded in the image, when saving or sharing.

4. Memo history and settings

Memos used in successfully saved photos are kept on your device as a history of up to 50 entries. Repeated memo text is consolidated into the latest entry. You can delete individual entries or clear all history in the history screen. If a deletion or save error appears, retry and check that the change was saved.

Note appearance and position, date display, caption preferences and other settings are also stored on your device. The developer does not retrieve this history or these settings. Deleting history does not remove memos already embedded in photos or captions in the standard photo library.

5. Captions and metadata (not finalized)

The development version includes a feature to register the entered memo as a photo caption on supported OS versions. Automatic registration can be disabled in the relevant settings screen. Inclusion in the release, supported OS versions, defaults and save-failure behavior will be reviewed before release.

When you share a photo with another app or person, memos embedded in the image and metadata such as captions may also be shared. Turning off automatic registration does not remove previously saved captions or embedded memos. Manage photos and metadata yourself using the standard Photos app or other appropriate tools.

6. Advertising (planned)

Google AdMob advertising is planned. The reviewed source does not include the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The following describes Google’s general practices relevant after integration, not data collection by an advertising SDK in the current app.

Google explains that its ad SDK may process device or app identifiers, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses and inferred approximate location, ad impressions and interactions, usage and diagnostics. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, analytics and service improvement. Actual fields will be checked against the integrated SDK, settings, region, permissions and consent.

Personalized ads use information such as past activity to select ads. Non-personalized ads may still involve frequency control, reporting and fraud-prevention processing; they do not mean no data is processed. The advertising approach for this app has not been finalized.

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The final policy will be updated with the actual SDKs, recipients and data fields.

7. Tracking and consent (being designed)

Integration and configuration of App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) are not finalized and are not implemented in the current source. Required permission and consent flows, ways to change choices and any privacy-options entry point will be described before release based on the final implementation. We do not represent unavailable consent screens or settings buttons as available.

Our policy is to keep basic features such as capture, memo compositing and saving available when tracking is declined. Camera and photo-add permission needed for capture and saving are separate from advertising or tracking permission.

8. Retention, deletion and backups

History is retained up to the 50-entry limit; older entries beyond the limit and user-deleted entries are removed from the history list. Settings remain on the device until changed, reset or otherwise removed. Deleting the app generally removes its local history and settings. This differs from OS actions such as offloading the app that preserve app data.

Photos saved to the standard photo library remain after the app is deleted. Delete them using the standard Photos app. Manage Recently Deleted items and copies at sharing destinations in their respective locations. The developer has no function to remotely delete photos from your device.

OS and iCloud backup, sync and restoration depend on your device and service settings. The current implementation marks the memo-history folder as excluded from backup, but the app does not manage or erase all backups or restorations of settings and photos. After advertising integration, Google’s retention and deletion practices will apply to data processed by Google.

9. Third-party services and this webpage

Apple’s and other providers’ practices apply to the photo library, OS sync and backups, and sharing destinations you choose. The app has no proprietary cloud sync. If ad SDKs or other services are added, we will review their providers and data practices and update this policy.

No advertising SDK, analytics or custom tracking cookies are added to this policy page. For communication information processed in serving the page through GitHub Pages, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Privacy choices and children’s information

You can change camera and photo-add permissions in device settings and review or delete history in the app. Manage photos and captions in Photos or other appropriate tools. Contact us about access, correction, deletion or other requests under applicable law. This does not mean the developer can retrieve or delete on-device data it does not hold.

The app has no feature designed to send children’s personal information to the developer for collection. Minors should review device permissions and sharing settings with a parent or guardian where appropriate. Parents or guardians may contact us about concerns regarding children’s information. Advertising age treatment and child-directed settings will be reviewed during ad integration and before release.
