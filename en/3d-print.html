3D PRINTING

Custom 3D Printing

Bring your ideas to life with custom 3D printing for personal projects. We review your intended use and model data to discuss feasibility and specifications.

What we can help with

  • 3D printing from your model files
  • Custom-made parts
  • Models and display items
  • Cosplay props and accessories for general audiences
  • Replacement parts
  • Small-batch prototyping

Specifications and quotes

Materials, print size, geometry, quantity, finish, pricing and lead times are discussed for each project. Some requests may fall outside our capabilities.

For replacement parts, please describe the operating environment and strength requirements. We cannot guarantee performance equivalent to the original part.

How to request a print

  1. Prepare your intended use, quantity, available model files and preferred deadline
  2. Discuss specifications and printing feasibility
  3. Review the quote and terms before placing your order

For personal 3D printing projects, please contact us through Coconala. Visit our Coconala service page to discuss your project.
